- project tittle: Customer & Sales Analysis Using SQL

- Project Overview

This project analyzes customer orders and sales data using SQL and MySQL.

The goal is to understand sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, regional performance, and monthly sales trends.

 Tools Used:

- MySQL
- XAMPP
- phpMyAdmin
- SQL

 Database Structure:The project contains three related tables;

- Customers
- Products
- Orders
These tables are connected using primary keys and foreign keys.

Business Questions:The analysis answers questions such as;

1. How many orders were placed?
2. What are the total sales?
3. Who are the highest-value customers?
4. Which products generate the most sales?
5. Which region generates the most sales?
6. What is the average order value?
7. Which months have the highest sales?
8. Are there customers who have not placed any orders?
9. How can orders be grouped based on sales value?
10. Which product categories perform best?

 SQL Skills Demonstrated:

- SELECT statements
- Aggregate functions
- COUNT()
- SUM()
- AVG()
- WHERE
- ORDER BY
- GROUP BY
- HAVING
- CASE statements
- INNER JOIN
- LEFT JOIN
- Subqueries
- Multi-table analysis

 Key Findings:
- Total Orders: 30
- Total Sales: ₦8,753,000
- Average Order Value: ₦291,766.67
- Top Customer: John Okafor — ₦1,380,000
- Top Product: Laptop — ₦2,450,000
- Top Region: Lagos — ₦3,320,000**
- Highest Sales Month: January — ₦1,870,000

Business Insights:
Customer Performance;
John Okafor generated the highest total sales, followed by Mary Adeyemi and Daniel Eze.
This shows that a small group of customers contributes significantly to overall sales.

Product Performance:
Laptops generated the highest sales, followed by Smartphones and Tablets.
The business should continue monitoring demand for these products and maintain sufficient stock.

Regional Performance:
Lagos generated the highest sales among the regions analyzed.
This suggests that Lagos represents an important market for the business.

 Monthly Performance:
January recorded the highest monthly sales, while May recorded the lowest.
Understanding these sales patterns can help with inventory planning and marketing activities.

Recommendations:
- Maintain sufficient stock of high-performing products.
- Focus more sales and marketing activities on strong-performing regions.
- Identify ways to increase purchases from lower-value customers.
- Investigate the reasons for weaker sales months.
- Use customer and product performance data to support future business decisions.

Project Outcome:
This project demonstrates my ability to use SQL to work with relational databases, combine data from multiple tables, perform business analysis, and turn raw data into useful business insights.
